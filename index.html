A house built around the table

Dar Zaytoun means "house of the olive tree". The idea is simple: a welcoming dining room where recipes are cooked slowly, spices are ground fresh and nobody is rushed.

Our menu follows northern Moroccan traditions, with olive oil, preserved lemon, saffron and plenty of fresh herbs. Every dish is made to be shared.

  • Fresh bread baked daily
  • Vegetarian dishes on every section of the menu
  • Private tables for families and groups
